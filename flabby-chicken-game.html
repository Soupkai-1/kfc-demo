HOW TO PLAY

Tap or click to flap. Fly through the KFC buckets. Every bucket = 1 point. Hit a bucket = game over — unless you have a 🛡️ SHIELD!

🛡️ SHIELD POWER

Grab the floating 🛡️ SHIELD to become invincible for 6 seconds. Fly straight into a bucket to SMASH it! You can destroy as many buckets as you can reach before the shield expires.

🌙 NIGHT MODE

Reach score 5 and the sky turns to night — moon, stars, and all!
